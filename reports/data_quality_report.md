**Conclusion.** `image_type` is perfectly balanced (each lesion has both views), so it carries no label information and is safe to use. `image_manipulation == altered` is strongly enriched in Indeterminate lesions (about 13% vs 2%), so it is a potential shortcut: it is not given to the model and later results will also be reported on unaltered images only. `diagnosis_confirm_type` separates Benign from Malignant almost perfectly because it records whether a biopsy was done, i.e. a decision taken *after* a clinician suspected malignancy - a textbook label leak.
